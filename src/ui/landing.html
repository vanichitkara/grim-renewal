How it works

Three crew members share the work. You stay in charge.

  1. 1

    Scout

    Finds your receipts and billing emails, and works out who is charging you.

  2. 2

    Coroner

    Scores how forgotten each one is. The more forgotten, the more it rots.

  3. 3

    Reaper

    Cancels while you watch. Every step waits for your approval.

What lurks inside

Five ways the dead get put to rest.

  • The Reaper

    Cancels a subscription for you. You approve each step first.

  • The Graveyard

    Free trials count down in days. Bury them before they charge you.

  • Revenants

    A charge that comes back after you cancelled. The zombie climbs out of its grave again. Preview in the demo.

  • Ghosts

    Every subscription gets a forgotten score, and the more forgotten it is, the more it rots. Finding dormant accounts that still hold your card is coming soon.

  • The Boss Door

    Phone-only cancels. The Reaper flees, and you get a call script and a chargeback letter.

Privacy

The Reaper only reads receipts.

  • Read-only Gmail access. It can never send, edit or delete.
  • Raw email text is thrown away after the key facts are taken.
  • Nothing is sold or shared.
